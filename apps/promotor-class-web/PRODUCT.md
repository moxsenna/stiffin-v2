# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are Promotors / Practitioners (STIFIn promoters, parenting educators, counselors, career coaches, and professional service creators in Indonesia) who run education sessions, assessment programs, workshops, and coaching. Secondary users are Learners (parents, professionals, students) who register and consume the learning material on mobile web / desktop.

## Product Purpose

PromotorClass is a Client Education OS that turns a promoter's knowledge into high-converting lead magnets, structured aftersales onboarding, private client portals, upsell funnels, and paid programs. It closes the disconnect between learning activity (video watching, reflection completion) and business follow-up (WhatsApp triggers, high-intent lead signals, upsell triggers).

## Positioning

Unlike generic LMS platforms (Teachable, Kajabi, Moodle) that stop at completion percentages, and unlike cold CRM automations, PromotorClass translates learning behavioral signals into immediate actionable human follow-ups via WhatsApp and PromotorFlow.

## Operating Context

Promotors operate largely via WhatsApp and live workshops in Indonesia. They need fast, mobile-friendly landing pages with frictionless WhatsApp-first / magic-link registrations that deliver bite-sized lessons and capture real-time intent.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Responsive Next.js App Router, fast Cloudflare edge-compatible static/SSR.
- Clean Modernist / Quiet Utility design system (#286344 emerald brand accent, #F7F7F5 warm canvas, high-legibility typography, crisp authored SVGs).
- Public Storefronts (`/p/[workspaceSlug]`), Program Detail Sales Pages (`/p/[workspaceSlug]/[programSlug]`), and Platform Landing Page (`/`).
- Frictionless registration with instant access to learning materials.
- Direct-response conversion components: interactive preview, social proof, clear value comparisons, actionable WhatsApp CTA.

## Brand Commitments

- Brand Name: PromotorClass
- Tone of Voice: Professional, human, trustworthy, pragmatic, direct, calm authority.
- Color: Deep Emerald `#286344`, Forest `#1f5238`, Canvas `#F7F7F5`, Warm Off-White, Charcoal text `#191918` / `#20211F`.
- Aesthetics: Quiet Utility, editorial typography with Georgia / serif emphasis on headlines where appropriate, balanced whitespace, zero generic AI slop.

## Product Principles

1. **Education as a Lifecycle Engine**: Learning is not a siloed product; it is the most effective conversion and retention vehicle.
2. **Quiet Utility over Gimmicks**: Structure should be felt, not constantly decorated with unnecessary cards or gradient slop.
3. **Frictionless Mobile-First Experience**: High speed, zero cognitive load, native-feeling touch targets on mobile.
4. **Actionable Business Signals**: Every lesson view and reflection connects directly to promoter follow-up.
